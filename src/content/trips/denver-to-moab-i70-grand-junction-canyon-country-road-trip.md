**Denver to Moab via I-70 and US-191 covers approximately 370 miles and 6–7 hours of total driving time, best split across two days. This is the definitive canyon-country drive from the Front Range: high desert, carved sandstone, and one of the most dramatic interstate corridors in the United States.**

Most Denver-to-Moab guides send you straight down US-6 or rush you past everything west of Grand Junction. This itinerary treats the drive itself as the destination — Glenwood Canyon alone is worth leaving early for — and gives you a full morning in Moab before the summer heat peaks.

## Quick Facts

- **Total distance:** ~370 miles
- **Total drive time:** 6–7 hours (recommended over 2 days)
- **Best season:** Late spring and early fall; summer is doable with early starts
- **Number of stops:** 5 major stops
- **Starting point:** Denver, CO
- **Ending point:** Moab, UT

## Who This Route Is For

- **Ideal for first-time Southwest visitors** who want to connect Colorado mountain scenery with Utah red-rock landscapes in one logical corridor
- **Good for car campers and van builds** — campgrounds at Fruita and Moab accommodate most setups; Grand Junction has full hook-up RV parks
- **Not recommended for drivers who skip fuel planning** — the stretch from Green River, UT to Moab on US-191 is 52 miles with no services
- **Works well for families** who want one national park (Arches) plus easy hikes without requiring technical gear

## Denver, CO — Starting Point

Leave Denver by 7:00 a.m. on I-70 West. Summer traffic through the Eisenhower Tunnel (11,158 ft) builds fast on weekends; Colorado Department of Transportation frequently posts chain restrictions and delays here even in June during late-season storms. Check CDOT's cotrip.org before departure. Fill your tank in Denver — gas is cheaper here than anywhere along the route.

## Glenwood Canyon Overlook *(approx. 2 hrs 45 min from Denver)*

I-70 enters Glenwood Canyon around mile marker 116, and for the next 12 miles the highway is literally carved into the cliff face above the Colorado River. Pull off at the Hanging Lake Trailhead rest area (mile marker 125) to stretch and take in the canyon walls. **Summer 2026 note:** The Hanging Lake Trail requires advance permits from Recreation.gov; day-of permits are rarely available. Book 30+ days out or plan to skip the trail and enjoy the canyon from the road and river trail.

## Glenwood Springs, CO *(15 min from the canyon overlook)*

Glenwood Springs is your first real stop. Eat at Slope & Hatch (729 Grand Ave), a reliable breakfast and burrito spot that opens at 7:30 a.m. The Glenwood Hot Springs Pool is the world's largest mineral hot springs pool — worth an hour if you're not rushing. Top off your gas tank here; it's significantly cheaper than Grand Junction and far cheaper than anything in Utah.

## Grand Junction, CO *(approx. 1 hr from Glenwood Springs)*

Grand Junction sits at the junction of I-70 and US-50 and is the last major city before the Utah border. Stock up on groceries at City Market on Horizon Drive if you're camping in Moab. The city itself is a solid overnight stop — the Springhill Suites and several independent motels cluster near the I-70 Business Loop. Grand Junction is also the gateway to the Colorado National Monument.

## Colorado National Monument *(20 min from downtown Grand Junction via CO-340)*

Rim Rock Drive (CO-340) is a 23-mile scenic road through Ute Canyon, Monument Canyon, and Red Canyon — all accessible by standard passenger vehicles. The drive takes 45–60 minutes without stops. Otto's Trail (0.9 miles round trip) gives you a canyon-rim view with minimal effort. Monument Road entrance station charges $25 per vehicle; America the Beautiful passes accepted. Summer temperatures on the monument top out near 100°F by noon — arrive by 8:00 a.m. or after 5:00 p.m.

## Green River, UT *(approx. 1 hr 15 min from the monument via I-70 West)*

Cross into Utah on I-70 West. This stretch of I-70 between the Utah border and Green River is one of the emptiest highways in the lower 48 — 100 miles with almost no services. **Fill up in Fruita, CO (just west of Grand Junction) before this segment.** Green River is a small town but has two gas stations, a Motel 6, and Ray's Tavern (25 S Broadway) — a genuinely good burger joint that's been feeding river runners and road trippers since 1941. From Green River, take US-191 South.

## Moab, UT *(approx. 52 min from Green River via US-191 South)*

US-191 South from Green River drops you into Moab past the northern entrance of Arches National Park. **Timed entry reservations for Arches are required from April through October in 2026** — book at Recreation.gov before your trip. Entry windows fill weeks in advance. The park entrance fee is $35 per vehicle. For the best light and the coolest temperatures, enter at 7:00 a.m. The Windows Section and Delicate Arch Trail (3 miles round trip, 480 ft gain) are both accessible to most hikers. Downtown Moab has no shortage of restaurants; Moab Diner (189 S Main St) is open early and serves solid diner food before you head into the park.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to drive from Denver to Moab?

The direct route via I-70 West and US-191 South is approximately 370 miles and takes about 5.5–6 hours of pure driving time. We recommend splitting it into two days with an overnight in Grand Junction or Glenwood Springs to allow time at Colorado National Monument and an early start into Arches.

### Is I-70 through Glenwood Canyon safe to drive in summer?

Glenwood Canyon is generally safe in summer, but the canyon closes several times per year due to rockfall, flooding, or debris flows. In June and July, afternoon thunderstorms can trigger closures with little notice. Check CDOT's cotrip.org the morning of your drive and have a backup route via CO-82 and US-50 through Montrose in mind.

### Do you need a permit to enter Arches National Park?

Yes. Arches National Park requires timed entry reservations from April 1 through October 31. Reservations are available at Recreation.gov and typically open 90 days in advance. A small number of same-day reservations are released at 6:00 a.m. MT, but they go fast during summer peak season.

### Where should you stop for gas on the Denver to Moab drive?

Fill up in Denver before departure, again in Glenwood Springs (cheapest prices on the corridor), and again in Fruita, CO before crossing into Utah. The stretch of I-70 from the Utah border to Green River has essentially no fuel options, and Green River to Moab on US-191 is another 52 miles. Don't rely on finding gas between Fruita and Green River.

### What is the best time of year to drive from Denver to Moab?

Late April through early June and mid-September through October offer the best combination of mild temperatures, open roads, and manageable crowds. Summer (June–August) is the busiest period — Arches timed entry slots sell out, and midday temperatures in Moab regularly exceed 100°F. Winter travel is possible but Glenwood Canyon and mountain passes on I-70 can close due to snow and ice.

### Can you do Denver to Moab in one day?

Technically yes — the drive is under 6 hours on I-70 and US-191. But doing it in one day means skipping Glenwood Canyon, Colorado National Monument, and arriving in Moab too late to enter Arches at a reasonable hour. Two days is the practical minimum for this route to be worth the trip.
